# Beginner Roadmap

Starting Block Blast can feel straightforward, but building long-term consistency requires more than placing pieces wherever they fit. This roadmap introduces the habits that help new players improve steadily without relying on trial and error.

## Stage 1: Learn the Basics

During your first few games, focus on understanding how each move affects the available space on the board rather than trying to achieve a high score.

Goals:

- Learn how different pieces fit together.
- Avoid rushing decisions.
- Become familiar with common board layouts.
- Observe how the board changes after every placement.

For a complete introduction to gameplay mechanics and beginner concepts, see:

[Block Blast Gameplay Guide](https://blockblastdownload.com/game-guide/)

---

## Stage 2: Improve Board Awareness

Once the controls feel comfortable, begin paying attention to board structure instead of individual moves.

Key habits include:

- Keeping the center of the board open.
- Avoiding isolated empty spaces.
- Preserving large connected areas.
- Leaving multiple placement options available.

Players who consistently maintain flexible board layouts usually survive much longer than players who only chase immediate points.

---

## Stage 3: Build Consistency

Consistency is often more valuable than aggressive scoring.

Practice:

- Looking ahead before placing a piece.
- Comparing several possible moves.
- Choosing placements that leave future options open.
- Recovering calmly after mistakes.

Improvement usually comes from reducing avoidable errors rather than discovering hidden tricks.

---

## Stage 4: Learn Recovery

Every player eventually reaches a difficult board position.

Instead of restarting immediately, practice recovering by:

- Creating additional open space.
- Removing unnecessary pressure.
- Clearing rows or columns whenever practical.
- Prioritizing flexibility over score.

Developing recovery skills often produces larger improvements than learning new scoring techniques.

---

## Stage 5: Continue Learning

After building confidence with the fundamentals, explore more advanced topics such as:

- High score planning
- Solver concepts
- Space management
- Long-term board control
- Advanced placement strategies
