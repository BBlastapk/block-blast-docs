# Advanced Play

Advanced Block Blast gameplay is built on consistency rather than speed. Experienced players spend more time evaluating the board than reacting to the next available move.

This guide focuses on long-term decision making and board control rather than individual scoring opportunities.

## Think Beyond the Current Piece

Every placement changes the available space for future pieces.

Before placing a piece, consider:

- How much open space will remain?
- Will the move create isolated gaps?
- Does it improve future flexibility?
- Can the board recover if a difficult piece appears next?

Planning several moves ahead usually produces better results than maximizing immediate points.

---

## Control Board Pressure

Board pressure increases as usable space becomes limited.

Common signs include:

- Crowded center
- Narrow placement lanes
- Multiple isolated gaps
- Few remaining placement options

Reducing board pressure should become a higher priority than increasing score.

---

## Preserve Flexible Areas

Large connected spaces allow more placement combinations than fragmented boards.

Whenever possible:

- Keep several placement paths available.
- Avoid dividing the board into small sections.
- Protect central space.
- Delay difficult placements until they become necessary.

---

## Manage Risk

Not every scoring opportunity is worth taking.

Experienced players often sacrifice a small number of points if it helps preserve a healthier board for later turns.

This approach usually produces more consistent long-term performance.

---

## Recover From Mistakes

Every game eventually reaches difficult positions.

Instead of forcing risky moves:

- Look for placements that reopen space.
- Remove board pressure gradually.
- Restore flexibility before chasing additional points.
- Accept smaller gains when they improve future options.

Recovery is one of the most valuable long-term skills.

---

Advanced players continually review their own gameplay to identify repeated mistakes and improve future decision making.
