# Gameplay Glossary

This glossary explains common gameplay terms used throughout the documentation. Understanding these concepts makes strategy guides and technical references easier to follow.

---

## Board Pressure

The amount of stress placed on the board when available placement space becomes limited.

High board pressure usually reduces future placement options.

---

## Board Control

Maintaining enough usable space to place a wide variety of pieces without creating unnecessary risk.

Good board control generally leads to longer games.

---

## Combo

A sequence of successful clears that keeps the board organized while creating additional space.

---

## Dead Cell

A single empty square or isolated gap that becomes difficult or impossible to use efficiently.

Dead cells often reduce placement flexibility.

---

## Flexible Space

Large connected open areas that can accommodate many different piece shapes.

Flexible space is one of the most valuable resources during a game.

---

## Forced Move

A placement with very few practical alternatives because the board has become crowded.

Frequent forced moves usually indicate increasing board pressure.

---

## Open Center

Keeping the central area of the board relatively clear so future pieces have more placement options.

Many experienced players try to preserve an open center for as long as possible.

---

## Placement Efficiency

Making moves that balance immediate progress with long-term board flexibility.

Efficient placements usually avoid creating isolated gaps or restricting future choices.

---

## Recovery

The process of improving a difficult board position by restoring usable space and reducing pressure.

Recovery often requires several careful moves rather than one large scoring opportunity.

---

## Risk Management

Evaluating whether a move improves or weakens future board conditions.

Sometimes accepting a smaller immediate score creates better long-term results.

---

## Space Management

Planning placements to maximize usable board area throughout an entire game.

Strong space management helps delay difficult board states.

---

## Strategy

A long-term approach to gameplay rather than a single move or tactic.

Effective strategies usually focus on consistency instead of short-term scoring.
