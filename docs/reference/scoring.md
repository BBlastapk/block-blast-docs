# Scoring Fundamentals

Scores increase through efficient placement and consistent board management. High scores generally come from maintaining flexibility over long sessions rather than relying on individual high-value moves.

## Factors That Influence Scores

- Board stability
- Consecutive clears
- Efficient placement
- Reduced dead space
- Long-term planning

## Consistency Over Speed

Many experienced players deliberately slow down to evaluate multiple placements before making a move.
