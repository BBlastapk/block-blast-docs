# Board Management

Board management is the practice of preserving usable space throughout a game. Experienced players often evaluate the long-term effect of every placement rather than focusing only on immediate line clears.

## Core Principles

### Keep Large Areas Connected

Large connected spaces provide more flexibility for future pieces.

### Avoid Isolated Cells

Single empty cells surrounded by blocks often become impossible to use efficiently.

### Protect the Center

An open center usually allows more placement options than crowded edges.

### Balance Risk

Sometimes accepting a smaller score now prevents much larger problems later.

## Common Mistakes

- Filling corners too early.
- Creating narrow vertical gaps.
- Leaving isolated empty squares.
- Playing only for immediate points.
