# Gameplay Glossary

## Board Pressure

A situation where available placement space becomes increasingly limited.

## Dead Cell

An isolated empty square that is difficult or impossible to use efficiently.

## Flexible Space

Large connected open areas that accommodate multiple piece shapes.

## Recovery Move

A move intended to restore board flexibility rather than maximize immediate points.

## Combo

Multiple successful line clears created in close succession.

## Placement Efficiency

Choosing positions that preserve future options while maintaining a stable board.
