# Solver Concepts

Puzzle solvers evaluate possible placements and compare future board states. They are useful for studying decision-making rather than replacing player judgment.

## Typical Evaluation Factors

- Remaining space
- Future flexibility
- Line clear opportunities
- Risk of blocked areas
- Piece compatibility

No solver can guarantee the highest possible score because future pieces introduce uncertainty into every game.
